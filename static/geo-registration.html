Geo-Fenced Registration

Verified on-site check-in for conferences, offices, and field events — registration that only works when guests are physically at the venue.

Attendees enter their name and email, pick a predefined location, and the app validates their GPS position against a 500m geofence before unlocking the camera. Each selfie is stamped with date, time, location name, and coordinates — then saved with server-side re-validation so records stay trustworthy.

How it works

  1. Register with name & email
  2. Select a venue location
  3. GPS checked — must be within 500m
  4. Capture a selfie with timestamp & location overlay
  5. Upload & save to the database
