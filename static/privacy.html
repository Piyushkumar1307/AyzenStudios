Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026. This policy explains how Ayzen Studios collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, and password hash when you register.
  • Authentication data: OTP verification records and sign-in tokens needed to secure your account.
  • Gameplay data: scores, leaderboard entries, unlocked games, and related in-game progress metadata.
  • Payment metadata: transaction references and unlock status (we do not store your full card details).

2. Camera and gesture processing

Our games use camera-based interaction. Gesture or pose detection is primarily processed in your browser session for gameplay. We do not intentionally store raw camera video streams as part of normal gameplay operation.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To authenticate logins and secure access.
  • To provide game access, purchases, leaderboards, and support.
  • To detect abuse, fraud, duplicate unlock issues, and service misuse.
  • To improve reliability and fix technical problems.

4. Sharing of data

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with service providers needed to run the platform, such as payment processors or hosting providers, under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

5. Data retention

We retain account and transaction-related records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request account deletion by contacting support.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we encourage strong passwords and responsible account practices.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You may request correction or deletion of your account data, subject to legal or security exceptions.
  • You may disable camera permission in your browser, though gesture-controlled games may not function.
  • You may contact us regarding privacy requests or questions.

8. Children

The service is not intentionally directed at children below the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us to review and remove it where appropriate.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date.
